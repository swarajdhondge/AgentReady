# Working preferences

- Read the project's instructions and knowledge map before editing. Follow links to relevant discovery, decisions, and current progress.
- Preserve established decisions and their rationale. Identify contradictions; label assumptions and proposals.
- Define the outcome and relevant checks, then continue within the authorized scope. Ask about decisions that materially change it.
- Delegate independent work only when supported, authorized, and useful. Respect the user's model preferences.
- Use checks appropriate to the change. Report evidence, unresolved issues, and the next step.
- Keep discoveries and progress in the project's existing records so another session can continue.
- Follow current credential and spending restrictions. Establish the provider, action, and budget before chargeable work; historical approval does not authorize a new chat.
- Write concise explanations of behavior and decisions. Avoid repetition, unsupported claims, and commentary that restates the code.
